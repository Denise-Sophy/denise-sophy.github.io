About me

Building AI systems
businesses run on

I'm an AI Engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya, currently leading the end-to-end development of Akili — a Claude-powered operations platform integrating Slack, Fireflies, and Google Workspace across a live 15-channel workspace for Romel Ventures.

My work spans agent architecture, RAG-based knowledge retrieval, context engineering, and multi-step AI workflows — translating business requirements into production systems for real clients, including AI advisory engagements for portfolio companies.

That AI work is backed by a security-conscious foundation: a Diploma and BSc in Information Technology from JKUAT, internships at ICT Authority and Kenya Broadcasting Corporation, and hands-on cloud security training through CyberShujaa. I design AI and cloud solutions with security built in, not bolted on.

I don't just build AI systems — I think about what happens when they meet production. IAM · data handling · cloud security · threat detection · secure architecture.
